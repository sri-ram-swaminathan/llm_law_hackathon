# Wealthpilot SAS: Business Plan 2026–2028

Version 1.0 · October 2026 · Confidential, prepared for the seed round (updated for the 1.0 release)

## 1. Summary

Wealthpilot gives French retail investors a personal investment adviser in their pocket. Users answer a short questionnaire, enter the investments they already hold, and get **personalised buy, sell, hold and rebalance recommendations on named instruments**, generated by AI in seconds and refreshed whenever they want.

Traditional advice in France is expensive, slow and aimed at wealthy clients. A *conseiller en investissements financiers* typically charges €150–€300 per meeting or takes a percentage of assets. Wealthpilot delivers the same personalised guidance at a fraction of the cost, at any time.

We are raising **€800k** to launch publicly in France in Q1 2027 and to reach 25,000 registered users by the end of 2027.

## 2. Problem

- 9 million French households hold securities, PEA or assurance-vie accounts, but fewer than 1 in 5 have ever had personalised advice.
- Advice is priced for portfolios above €100k. Younger investors with €5k–€50k are left with generic content, forums and influencers.
- Existing apps show performance but don't tell users **what to do next**.

## 3. Product

The product is live as a working prototype (see `docs/PRODUCT_GUIDE.md` and `docs/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`).

1. **Suitability onboarding.** Nine questions: age, income, goal, monthly budget, risk tolerance and horizon, plus investment knowledge, investment experience and the maximum loss the client can bear. No advice is generated until the profile is complete, and every analysis includes a suitability statement. If the client changes their profile, earlier analyses are flagged as out of date.
2. **Portfolio.** Users type in their holdings. Live prices come from public market data.
3. **AI recommendations.** On demand, Wealthpilot produces three concrete recommendations, each naming a specific instrument (for example *"Buy CW8.PA, 40% of new monthly contributions"* or *"Sell 30% of your Airbus position"*), plus a target allocation and a risk warning. Recommendations are tailored to the user's profile and portfolio.
4. **Dashboard.** Value, gains, a measured risk score compared with the user's stated tolerance, and market context.

What we don't do: execute trades, hold client money or securities, or take commissions from product providers.

## 4. Regulatory positioning

Wealthpilot provides **personalised investment advice** and is registered as a *conseiller en investissements financiers* (CIF) with ORIAS (no. 00000000, fictional), as a member of an AMF-approved CIF association. Its advice is non-independent. Every recommendation screen shows this status disclosure; see `compliance/cif-registration.md`.

The compliance lead owns the yearly registration, association membership, training and complaint handling. The CCOmmit compliance check runs on every release candidate.

The terms of service and privacy policy are published (`compliance/terms.md`, `compliance/privacy-policy.md`) and are accepted at sign-up.

## 5. Business model

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 | Portfolio tracking, risk score, 1 AI analysis per month |
| Premium | €9.99 / month | Unlimited AI analyses, rebalancing suggestions, priority support |

We take no commissions and no payments from product providers. Target conversion from free to premium: 6% in year 1, 9% in year 2.

## 6. Market and competition

- **Target users:** French residents aged 25–45, investable assets of €5k–€100k, using PEA, assurance-vie or a compte-titres.
- **Robo-advisers** (discretionary mandates) require transferring the money, and their fees are 0.75–1.5% a year. We keep the user's existing accounts.
- **Portfolio trackers** show data but give no recommendations.
- **Human CIFs** are personal but expensive, and they don't scale.

## 7. Go-to-market

2. Q1 2027: public launch in France, with content partnerships with personal-finance creators.
3. Q3 2027: referral programme, and a partnership with one neo-bank to embed recommendations.

## 8. Operations and technology

- **Team:** two co-founders (product and engineering) and one contract developer. We'll hire a compliance lead after the seed round.
- **Stack:** Python/FastAPI backend, React frontend, PostgreSQL. AI analysis by Mistral AI (a French provider), with no email or account ID sent.
- **Hosting:** runs locally today. Planned production on AWS Paris (`eu-west-3`), described in `docs/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- **Data:** users' profile answers, holdings and every generated analysis are kept in our database. We don't collect names, addresses or bank credentials.

## 10. Key risks

- **Regulatory:** advice quality and suitability obligations under the CIF regime. *Mitigation:* the suitability questionnaire, suitability statements, advice history, the compliance lead's reviews, and the CCOmmit check on every release.
- **AI quality:** recommendations may be wrong or unsuitable. *Mitigation:* prefer diversified ETFs; output schema validation.
- **Market data:** we depend on a free, unofficial price source. *Mitigation:* move to a licensed feed before scaling.
