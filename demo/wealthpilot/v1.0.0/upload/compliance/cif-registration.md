## 2. What changes in the product

- The product presents its recommendations as **personalised investment advice provided under CIF status**. It no longer describes them as "not financial advice".
- Every recommendation screen shows a status disclosure: CIF status, ORIAS number, non-independent advice, based on the information the client provided, with a link to the terms.
- Before any advice is given, the client completes a **suitability questionnaire**. It covers knowledge and experience, financial situation including ability to bear losses, and investment objectives. Advice isn't generated while the profile is incomplete.
- Each analysis includes a **suitability statement** explaining why the advice suits the client. Clients can view every past analysis in their history.
- When the client's profile changes, existing advice is marked out of date until it's refreshed.
