# Wealthpilot: Terms of Service

Version 1.0 · Effective at public launch · Wealthpilot SAS, Paris, France (fictional)

## 1. Who we are

Wealthpilot SAS provides the Wealthpilot service. Wealthpilot is a *conseiller en investissements financiers* (CIF) registered with ORIAS under no. 00000000 (fictional), and a member of an AMF-approved CIF association. Details are in our CIF registration record.

## 2. The service

Wealthpilot provides **personalised investment advice** on financial instruments, based on the information you give us:
- your investor profile: knowledge and experience, financial situation including the losses you can bear, and objectives and horizon;
- the holdings you enter.

Our advice is **non-independent**. We receive no commissions or payments from the providers of the instruments we mention, but we don't analyse a sufficiently wide range of instruments to call our advice independent.

Wealthpilot does **not**:
- execute orders, hold your money or securities, or connect to your bank or broker;
- give tax or legal advice. Mentions of PEA, assurance-vie or compte-titres are general.

## 3. How advice is produced

Recommendations are generated by an AI model (Mistral AI) from your profile and holdings, within rules we set and review. Each analysis includes a **suitability statement** explaining why the recommendations fit your profile.

We won't give advice if your profile is incomplete, or if the recommendation wouldn't be suitable for you. If you change your profile, your previous analysis is marked out of date until you refresh it.

AI output can be wrong. Investing involves risk, and you can lose some or all of the money you invest.

## 4. Your account

- You must be at least 18 and resident in France.
- Keep your password confidential. You're responsible for activity on your account.
- You can **delete your account at any time** from the account menu. Deletion removes your profile, holdings and analyses (see the Privacy Policy).
- You can view every analysis we've given you in your history.
