# Wealthpilot: Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective at public launch

## 1. Controller

Wealthpilot SAS, Paris, France (fictional), is the controller of the personal data processed through the Wealthpilot service. Contact: privacy@wealthpilot.example.

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, password (stored as a bcrypt hash) | Creating and securing your account | Performance of the contract (6(1)(b)) |
| Investor profile: age, income, objectives, monthly budget, risk tolerance, horizon, investment knowledge and experience, the losses you can bear | Assessing suitability and personalising advice | Performance of the contract (6(1)(b)); legal obligation to assess suitability (6(1)(c)) |
| Holdings: ticker, quantity, average buy price | Valuing your portfolio and personalising advice | Performance of the contract (6(1)(b)) |
| Analyses we generate for you | Providing advice and keeping your advice history | Performance of the contract (6(1)(b)) |
| Technical logs (IP address, request path, time) | Security and troubleshooting | Legitimate interest in securing the service (6(1)(f)) |

We don't collect your name, postal address, phone number, bank or brokerage credentials, or payment card data (payments go through our payment provider).

## 3. Automated processing

Your recommendations are generated automatically by an AI model from your profile and holdings. They're advice for you to consider, not decisions that produce legal effects on you. You remain free to act on them or not.

## 4. Recipients

| Recipient | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mistral AI (France) | Your profile and holdings, **without your email or account ID** | Generating the analysis |
| Yahoo Finance | Ticker symbols only, never data that identifies you | Market prices |
| Our hosting provider | All data, encrypted at rest, stored in the EU | Running the service |

We don't sell your data and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU

Your data is stored and processed in the European Union. We don't transfer it outside the EU.

## 6. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept until |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, holdings, analyses | You delete your account. Everything is deleted then. |
| Technical logs | 90 days |

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability.
- **Erasure:** delete your account at any time from the account menu. This deletes your profile, holdings and every analysis.
- For anything else, write to privacy@wealthpilot.example. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions are signed with a secret that must be configured in production, and the service won't start without it. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## 9. Changes

We'll inform you of material changes before they take effect.
